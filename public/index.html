About this demonstration

Candidate vetting is not planet discovery.

This site uses fixed outputs from the reproducible experiment: six held-out light-curve pairs, their recorded model scores, and their frozen NASA Exoplanet Archive dispositions. It does not query a live catalogue or claim that an individual score confirms a planet.
