A stricter second look at possible planets.

The project asks whether shape alone is enough, or whether a second stage of physical diagnostics can reject more convincing false alarms.

The question

Test whether a diagnostic second stage can reduce accepted false positives on observations from a newer TESS catalogue snapshot.

The approach

A two-view CNN reads global and transit-window light curves. Stage 2 adds period, depth, duration, BLS strength, odd/even differences and secondary-eclipse evidence.

The result

17 fewer accepted
false positives

False-positive rate fell from 0.604 to 0.486. Recall also fell from 0.851 to 0.716, so the improvement comes with a real temporal-generalisation trade-off.

Glossary

Transit
A small, temporary dimming when a planet crosses in front of its star.
Light curve
A record of how a star's measured brightness changes over time.
False positive
A signal that resembles a planet transit but has another cause.
TOI
A TESS Object of Interest: a signal selected for closer investigation.
BLS
Box Least Squares, a search for regularly repeating, transit-shaped dips.
Recall
The share of catalogue planets the model successfully keeps.
